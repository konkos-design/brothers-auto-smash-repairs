Smash & collision repairs

Panel beating & dent removal

Bumper repair & replacement

Bumpers are our most common job. Cracked, scuffed, pushed in or hanging off, we fix them quickly. You get a quote for repair and for replacement, so you can choose.

  • Clips, brackets and alignment fixed

Spray painting & resprays

Colour-matched paint for single panels, grilles, bumpers and complete resprays. We prep and paint each job carefully, and customers single out our attention to detail.

  • Single-panel and bumper painting

Chassis & structural repairs

Some collisions bend more than the panels. We inspect the structure under the bumper and quote chassis work clearly, so you know the full job and its cost before we start.

  • Panel gaps and alignment corrected
  • Clear, itemised quotes for repair vs replacement
