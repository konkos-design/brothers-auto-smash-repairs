About us

Brothers by name. Brothers by trade.

Brothers Auto Smash Repairs is a family workshop on Marsh Street in Clyde, close to Granville and Parramatta. It's run by Ali and his brothers.

Our trade is panel beating, spray painting and smash repairs, plus general car repairs. Customers usually meet Ali first: he looks at the car, explains what happened and gives a straight price. His brothers work beside him in the workshop. It's a small team, and the same people who quote your job are the ones who fix it.

We haven't built our name on advertising. It comes from 131 Google reviews, most of them from local drivers, and some bring us car after car. In those reviews people mention fair prices, quick turnaround, no upselling and a car handed back clean.

Good to know

Frequently asked

Do I need a booking, or can I just drop in?

Walk-ins are welcome and we'll look at your car straight away if we can. For bigger repairs, call ahead on +61 450 366 899 so we can plan the job.

Do you give a quote before starting?

Always. We explain the damage and give you a price first. If we find more work during the repair, we'll call you before doing it.

Repair or replace — who decides?

You do. Where a part like a bumper can be repaired or replaced, we'll explain both options and their prices so you can choose.

Do you do insurance work?

Yes, we do insurance and private repairs. Bring your claim details and we'll help with the process.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Thursday 9:30 am – 6:30 pm, Saturday 9:30 am – 3:00 pm. We're closed on Friday and Sunday.
